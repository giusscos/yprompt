# yPrompt — App Store Metadata v1.0.2

Goal of this version: more people tapping yPrompt in search results (above all on Mac, where only about 0.5% of searchers tap it) and the first trial starts.
Baseline numbers from launch (Jun 10) to Oct 1, 2026: 6.2K impressions · 89 product page views · 19 downloads · 0 ratings · 0 paying users.

Rules followed below:
- A word in the Name or Subtitle is never repeated in Keywords; Apple already indexes it.
- Keywords are comma-separated with no spaces, ≤ 100 characters.
- No competitor or trademark terms (YouTube, Zoom, etc.); App Review rejects them.
