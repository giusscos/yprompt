# yPrompt — App Store Metadata v1.0.5

Goal of this version: hand back finished videos and audio, and make recording free. Every take lands in a new Recordings tab, where it can be cut, trimmed, reordered, cleared of pauses, captioned in the script's own spelling, and continued from the last word said. Camera, audio and captions are free; free users' videos carry a small "Made with yPrompt" mark, and removing it is a reason to get Pro.

**Ship only after 1.0.4 has a clean trial-to-paid readout** from the Nov 7–10 check. Even then, App Store Connect can't separate the effect of captions from the free-recording change, since both ship together (decided 2026-11-06).

Rules followed below:
- Same terms and tone as 1.0.4: "textes"/vous (fr), "Skript"/du (de), "guion" (es), "copione" (it), "roteiro" (pt-BR), "naskah"/Anda (id).
- es-ES writes "vídeo", es-MX "video"; fr-CA uses the fr-FR text.
- These translations need a native speaker's review before upload.

## CHANGES IN THIS BUILD (1.0.5)

**Recordings tab**
- New tab between Scripts and Remote: recordings grouped by Today, This Week and Earlier, with thumbnails (a waveform icon for audio), length, number of pieces and a Saved badge. Swipe or long-press to delete, after confirming. An empty state explains how to record. A footer shows the space used and says recordings aren't backed up.
- Recordings live in `Application Support/Recordings/<id>/` (excluded from backup), as a `recording.json` plus the source files, not in SwiftData (which syncs through iCloud).
- Saving to Photos no longer deletes anything: the recording stays, marked Saved.
- A piece that was recording when the app closed is added the next time the tab or editor opens, if it can be played. Takes kept by earlier 1.0.5 test builds are imported.

**Editor** (opens after every take and from the tab)
- Preview with play/pause and a scrubber. A strip of pieces below, each as wide as its share of the length. Tap a piece to select it and jump there; drag a selected piece's yellow ends to trim; hold and drag to reorder.
- Split at the playhead, Delete, Add After (record a new piece there), and one level of Undo. Deleting the last piece asks to delete the recording.
- Remove Pauses: keeps only the spoken stretches of each piece (words less than 0.6 s apart are joined, 0.12 s kept either side). Without words it uses loudness.
- New pieces are trimmed to their first and last word automatically; the handles can bring the ends back.
- Captions (video): Off, Lyrics or Clean, Bottom or Middle; each piece is lined up with the script on its own. Rotate turns the whole recording.
- Transcript (audio): shown line by line in the preview.
- Save to Photos (video) or Share Audio. Share gives the video, or the `.m4a`, plus `.srt` subtitles and a `.txt` transcript.

**Editor controls** (after the first device test, 2026-11-06)
- No progress slider: the strip of pieces is the timeline, with a playhead knob to drag. Under the preview: play/pause in the middle, play from the start, and a preview speed (0.5×–2×), with the current time and length on either side.
- Trimming resizes the piece live as the handle moves. Splits, moves and deletes animate. Edits that don't change what plays (a split, a caption style) don't reload the preview. Captions are cached instead of redrawn every frame.
- Symbols change with symbol effects (play/pause, caption position, rotate, saved).
- Landscape: the recording and its transport on the left, the strip, tools and options on the right.

**Recording more**
- Record More (at the end) and Add After (after the selected piece) close the editor and put the reading screen at the word after the last one said in the piece before. A banner shows where the new piece will go; the next take goes there and the editor reopens on it. From the tab, this opens the reading screen for the recording's script.

- The pickup banner sits above the controls instead of over the lines under the camera, and shrinks to a small "Piece N" pill after 4 seconds or when recording starts; tap it to see it again. Switching to the other kind of recording (camera ↔ audio) ends the pickup.

**Under the Camera**
- A new slider in the text settings sets how many lines show under the camera, from 2 up to half the screen.

**Audio only**
- The reading screen's background menu has a third choice, Audio Only: the color background, with the big button recording from the microphone. It shares the mic with Voice Scroll like camera mode does. The record button's ring follows the controls' color, so it shows on light backgrounds.
- Starting and stopping the camera or microphone now runs on its own queue, one step at a time, and a new session waits for the old one to stop. Switching quickly between Camera, Audio Only and Color could stall the app before.

**Free and Pro**
- Camera, audio, captions, the tab and the editor are free.
- Free users' videos carry a small "Made with yPrompt" mark in the top-left corner, in the preview (with a Remove chip that opens the paywall) and in the saved video. Pro removes it.
- Pro list: "No yPrompt Mark" first; the Camera Recording and Captions rows are gone. Onboarding no longer marks the camera as Pro. New paywall headline when opened from the mark.

**How saving works**
- One stretch with nothing drawn on top: copied without re-encoding (a rotation only changes the orientation tag). Otherwise a custom compositor places each piece upright, rotated and filling the frame of the first piece, then draws captions and the mark. HEVC when any piece is HEVC. Audio: AppleM4A.
- Preview: the built-in compositor places the pieces; captions and the mark are drawn on top by the same renderer as the export.

**Earlier in 1.0.5**
- Script-spelled captions, Trim Silence at the ends, Rotate, the screen kept awake while reading, recording or reviewing, and takes started with the phone lying flat following the screen's orientation.

**Other**
- What's New on iPhone and iPad: Recordings, Edit in Seconds, Pick Up Where You Left Off, Captions, Audio Recordings, Recording Is Free. The Mac keeps the 1.0.4 sheet.
- Microphone and speech recognition permission texts mention recordings and captions.
- Debug: `-ypDemoTake <video>[,<video>…]` and `-ypDemoAudioTake <audio>` add files as a recording and open the editor.

**Tested in the simulator only**
- The simulator can't play a video composition (-12784) or output audio here (-12746), so the preview there falls back to the raw pieces and audio previews don't play. Saving was checked by reading frames from the saved videos: two pieces of different orientations, captions across pieces, the mark.
- Not yet on a real device: the editor preview with the layout, a real pickup with the camera and with audio only, Voice Scroll during audio recording, saving a long 4K recording, HDR, older iPhones.
