## CHANGES IN THIS BUILD (1.0.3)

- Teleprompter keeps the reading position when the text rewraps (fold/unfold, rotation, Split View) and keeps text clear of the side safe area.
- Split Screen camera mode on regular-width layouts (unfolded iPhone Duo, iPad), with Swap Sides.
- Camera settings: front/back camera, lens chips and zoom, 1080p/4K, 24/30/60 fps, stabilization, exposure compensation, focus/exposure lock, torch.
- Recording orientation fixed with `AVCaptureDevice.RotationCoordinator` (saved videos used to come out sideways).
- Not yet tested on a real device: back camera, lens switching, formats, torch, recording orientation.
