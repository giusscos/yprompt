### Description
```
YPrompt is the professional teleprompter for creators, speakers, and broadcasters. Whether you're recording a YouTube video, delivering a keynote, or hosting a podcast, YPrompt keeps your words flowing naturally — without anyone knowing you're reading a script.

——— FIVE SCROLL MODES ———
• Auto — Smooth continuous scroll at your chosen pace
• Tap — Advance each line manually with a single tap
• Voice — Hands-free scrolling that follows your natural speech rhythm
• Camera — Record professional video while your script scrolls in sync, invisible to your audience
• Timed — Auto-pace your delivery to hit a target duration every time

——— BUILT FOR EVERY APPLE DEVICE ———
YPrompt runs natively on iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple Watch. On Mac, a floating window hovers just below your webcam so you look straight into the lens while delivering every word. On iPhone, the music-player control panel gives you full control without breaking your flow.

——— FULL VISUAL CONTROL ———
Customize fonts, text size, colors, line height, text alignment, transparency, and mirror mode — per script. Adjust margins on the fly without stopping playback.

——— SCRIPT ORGANIZATION ———
Write from scratch, paste any text, or import a file. Organize scripts with tags, mark favorites, and search instantly. Build play queues to run multiple scripts back-to-back — perfect for long presentations or multi-segment shoots.

——— CUE POINTS ———
Drop markers anywhere in a script to jump between key sections instantly during a live session.

——— REMOTE CONTROL ———
Control your Mac teleprompter directly from your iPhone over Wi-Fi or Bluetooth. Or use your Apple Watch for subtle wrist-based control during live presentations — without touching your phone.

——— iCLOUD SYNC ———
All your scripts sync automatically across every device signed into your iCloud account.

——————————————

YPrompt Free includes up to 3 scripts with preset fonts and colors — no account required.

YPrompt Pro unlocks:
• Unlimited scripts
• Voice Scroll
• Camera Recording (video saved to your Photos library)
• Custom fonts & colors
• iCloud sync across all devices
• Every future feature — included

Upgrade with a monthly or yearly subscription, or get Lifetime access with a single one-time purchase.

Payment is charged to your Apple ID at purchase confirmation. Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled. Manage or cancel anytime in your Apple ID settings.
```
