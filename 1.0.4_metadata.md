## CHANGES IN THIS BUILD (1.0.4)

**Voice and reading**
- Voice Scroll recognizes speech and keeps your place word by word: it copes with misheard, skipped and added words, and follows you when you jump ahead or go back. If recognition isn't available (permission declined, unsupported language) it falls back to moving while you speak.
- Lyrics Style (Pro): words fill as they're read, the current line grows, and lines settle with a staggered spring. Upcoming lines stay sharp so you can read ahead.
- 1.0× now means 150 words a minute on every device, font size and window width. It was a fixed pixel speed: about 460 words a minute on iPhone and far faster in the Mac floating window.
- Scripts no longer "finish" while the last screenful is still unread.

**Camera**
- New Under the Camera layout, now the default: a few lines right under the Dynamic Island or notch over a clear preview, with recording time beside the island. One button starts recording and reading together.
- The app now sets up the audio session itself so Voice Scroll and recording can share the microphone.

**Reading screen (iPhone and iPad)**
- Replaced the player sheet with a few floating controls and a Camera-style mode strip you tap or swipe.
- The controls fade while the script plays; tap the script to bring them back. A thin progress line stays, with a tick at each cue point.
- Each mode shows only its own setting: speed for Auto, duration for Timed, a live mic level for Voice.
- Drag the script itself to move through it; the progress slider is gone. Hold the flag button to jump to a cue point.
- Switching between Color and Camera, and between camera layouts, is now one continuous fade instead of a cut.
- In camera mode the big button records, and back is disabled while recording, so a take can't be left by accident. The recording time shows beside the speed.
- Removed the "Slider Hold to Confirm" setting on iPhone, since no slider there uses it.

**Remembers your choices**
- Mode, background, camera layout and camera side, speed, text size, margins and Timed length now carry across scripts and launches. Customize choices become the starting style for new scripts, and existing scripts keep their own colors.
- On Mac: speed, text sizes, floating or notch, window look and playback mode.

**What's New sheet**
- People updating see a short list of what changed, once. New installs get the onboarding instead. Pro features carry a small PRO label for free users.

**Free and Pro**
- Locked for free users: Voice, Lyrics Style, Camera, Timed, more than 3 scripts, Mac notch, iPhone remote, colors beyond the first row, custom colors.
- Free: auto and tap scrolling, Mac floating window, font size, first row of colors, iCloud sync.
- The paywall lists only what's really locked. Duplicating a script no longer gets around the free limit.

**First run**
- The tour shows words lighting up on its first page and the Under the Camera layout on its camera page, and lists Lyrics Style. On Mac it no longer lists Camera.
- New installs start with a short sample script in lyrics style, opened and ready to play after the tour. It doesn't count towards the free limit.
- The paywall appears once, after the first finished read. Locked controls show a lock.
- New scripts open by themselves with the cursor ready, a hint, and Paste and Import buttons.
- The first lines are no longer hidden under the status bar and buttons.

**Mac**
- Locked features open the paywall in its own window instead of an alert that said to open the main app.
- Pro status now works when only the menu bar is used.
- Voice mode in the notch now stops when you stop speaking.

**Other**
- Removed the iCloud toggle and "Last synced" row, which never did anything.
- Turning on Voice while the script is playing now starts the microphone.
- App languages: added German, Portuguese (Brazil) and Indonesian, and filled in strings that were never translated.
