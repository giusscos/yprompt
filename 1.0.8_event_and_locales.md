## NEW STORE LOCALES — `en-GB`, `en-AU`, `en-CA`

Why: each English locale is another name, subtitle and 100-character keyword field, with no
translation risk. Same rules as before: no word from the name or subtitle repeated in keywords,
no spaces after commas, no competitor names. These are version fields, so they go live with the
next submitted version (1.0.9), not before.

Description, promotional text and What's New are copied from the live en-US 1.0.8 text; en-GB and
en-AU get British spelling (colour, customise, organise, favourite, centre…), en-CA keeps US spelling.
Screenshots are not uploaded: they fall back to en-US.

| Locale | Name | Subtitle | Keywords |
|---|---|---|---|
| `en-GB` | `yPrompt: Autocue Teleprompter` (29) | `Notch & Webcam Video Prompter` (29) | `script,speech,presenter,cue,cards,voice,scroll,reader,captions,subtitles,record,vlog,podcast,camera` (99) |
| `en-AU` | `yPrompt: Teleprompter & Script` (30) | `Autocue for Video & Webcam` (26) | `presenter,speech,cue,cards,voice,scroll,reader,captions,subtitles,record,vlog,podcast,camera,notch` (98) |
| `en-CA` | `yPrompt: Teleprompter & Script` (30) | `Notch & Webcam Video Prompter` (29) | `autocue,presenter,speech,cue,cards,voice,scroll,reader,captions,subtitles,record,vlog,podcast,notes` (99) |

"Autocue" is the everyday British word for a teleprompter, so en-GB carries it in the name.
en-AU differs from en-GB on purpose: where a storefront reads both, they add up instead of repeating.
