# yPrompt — App Store Metadata v1.0.7

Goal of this version: ship four fixes found right after 1.0.5 and 1.0.6, and give the en-US keywords their first update since 1.0.2, so they describe what the app does now (recording, captions, editing, audio). 1.0.7 is the third release in ten days, so it adds no new feature and no in-app What's New sheet.

The four fixes, all on `main` since 1.0.6 (`git log fe8221c..HEAD`):
1. **Videos without sound save and share again** (bd3a115). When no piece of a recording had sound, the export stopped with "Operation Stopped" and the video could not be saved to Photos or shared. Free users always hit it, because adding the "Made with yPrompt" mark re-encodes the video. Recordings with sound are unchanged.
2. **The editor's rotate button moved** (fdf7195) from the top right to the bottom right of the preview. On a tall video, the "Made with yPrompt" mark and its Remove button span the top, and the rotate button covered the end of Remove.
3. **A rating request after a video is saved to Photos** (1ebdbd2). Until now the app only asked after a script was read to the end, and the first such read shows the paywall instead, so most people were never asked. It now asks about a second after "Saved to Photos", never over the paywall, the share sheet or a dialog, and at most once every 60 days.
4. **Pro ends when the billing grace period ends** (633e0f6). The grace period was turned on in App Store Connect on 2026-11-13 (16 days). Up to 1.0.6 the app also counted a subscription in billing retry as Pro, which Apple can keep retrying for up to 60 days.

Fixes 1–3 are iPhone and iPad only (recording and the editor don't exist on the Mac). Fix 4 applies to the Mac too.

Rules followed below:
- Same format, tone and keyword rules as `1.0.6_metadata.md`: a word in the Name or Subtitle is never repeated in Keywords; keywords are comma-separated with no spaces after commas, at most 100 characters; no competitor or brand names; counts are characters, as App Store Connect counts them.
- What's New uses each language's button names from its `Localizable.strings` (Rotate, Remove), so the store text matches what people see in the editor. It doesn't mention ratings, subscriptions or billing.
- es-ES writes "vídeos", es-MX "videos"; fr-CA uses the fr-FR text, as in 1.0.5 and 1.0.6.
- **These translations were machine-written and need a native speaker's review before upload.**
- Search ranks are from Apple's public search API (`itunes.apple.com/search`, US storefront, up to 200 results, 2026-11-13). They approximate the App Store's own ranking and move from day to day. Rating counts are US ratings as the API reports them.
- The in-app What's New sheet is unchanged: `WhatsNew.release` stays `"1.0.6"`, so people who saw the 1.0.6 sheet see nothing new, and people updating from 1.0.5 or older (and Mac users coming from 1.0.4) still see the 1.0.6 sheet once.

## OTHER KEYWORD FIELDS THE US STORE READS *(optional, lower confidence)*

**Which localizations the United States App Store indexes.** Apple doesn't document this. Third-party ASO tools publish lists from their own tests:
- aso.dev, "Cross-Localization" (https://aso.dev/aso/cross-localization/, page updated 2026-10-24): English (US) plus Arabic, Chinese (Simplified), Chinese (Traditional), French, Korean, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Spanish (Mexico) and Vietnamese.
- Appfigures, "How to 10x Your App Store Keyword List with Secondary Localizations" (https://appfigures.com/resources/guides/extend-keyword-list, table dated 2023-04-25): the same nine extra languages.
- AppFollow, "App Store keywords localizations" (https://appfollow.io/app-store-keywords-localizations, no date): English (US) and Spanish (Mexico) only.

How solid: all three agree on Spanish (Mexico), which has been reported for years. The longer list comes from two sources, one of them three years old, and it is not clear the 2026 page re-tested it rather than copying it. Treat es-MX as likely and the other eight as plausible. Of the 21 live locales, es-MX, fr-FR (assumed to be the "French" meant), pt-BR, ko, zh-Hans, zh-Hant, ar-SA and vi would be read by the US store; their live fields are in the readout (section E).

Two more caveats from the same sources: keywords in a secondary localization combine only with that localization's own name, subtitle and keywords, not with the en-US name, so a term like "teleprompter captions" works there only if both words are in that field; and a word repeated in two fields counts once.

**Where it costs least.** By the storefront numbers (Oct 1–Nov 12), Mexico (13 downloads, 1 paid Annual), Colombia (14) and Peru (9) use es-MX, and France (20 downloads, 4 trials) uses fr-FR, so those two fields stay local. China, Taiwan, Hong Kong, Korea, Brazil, Vietnam and the Arabic-speaking stores are not in the top 10 for impressions (below Colombia's 212) or downloads (below Peru's 9). The two Chinese fields also have the most unused room (zh-Hans 71 of 100 characters, zh-Hant 74), so English words fit there **without removing any Chinese term**. That makes them the cheapest choice; nothing local is lost.

The English words chosen are ones that didn't fit in en-US and have some evidence. "teleprompter" is repeated in each so the pair can match within the field (if Apple does in fact combine across localizations, it is wasted and can go).

## SUBTITLE

**Unchanged in 1.0.7:** `Notch & Webcam Video Prompter` (29).

The subtitle is shared by iOS and Mac, and the Mac brings two thirds of impressions (7,119 of 10,862 since Oct 1). "Notch" is the Mac's best word: 15th for "notch teleprompter" on the Mac (43rd on iPhone), and no iPhone change is worth risking that. ## TEST ON A DEVICE BEFORE SUBMITTING

On an iPhone (and an iPad if you have one), with the 1.0.7 build installed over 1.0.6. Use a free account (not Pro) unless a step says otherwise; that is the case where the sound bug always hit.

**1. Video without sound**
- [ ] Make a take that has no sound. Turning off microphone access won't do it: the camera only starts when both camera and microphone are allowed. A take gets no sound when the app can't attach the microphone, so try recording while a phone or FaceTime call is using it. *Not verified that this gives a silent take; if you know how the bug was first hit, use that. If you can't make one, write "couldn't reproduce" here rather than ticking it.*
- [ ] Open the take in Recordings and tap Save to Photos. You should see "Saved to Photos", and the video in Photos plays with the mark and no sound. Before the fix this stopped with "Operation Stopped".
- [ ] Tap Share on the same take: the share sheet opens with the video.
- [ ] Regression: record a normal take with your voice, save it to Photos, and check that the sound is there and in sync.

**2. Rotate button**
- [ ] Record a tall (portrait) video as a free user and open it in the editor. The "Made with yPrompt" mark and its Remove button sit at the top of the preview; the rotate button is now in the bottom-right corner and covers nothing.
- [ ] Tap Remove: the paywall opens. Tap the rotate button: the video turns 90°.
- [ ] Turn captions on (Lyrics or Clean): check that the rotate button doesn't sit on top of the caption text at the bottom. *Captions run along the bottom of the preview, so this is the one place the new position could get in the way.*
- [ ] Repeat with a landscape take.

**3. Rating request**
- [ ] Run the app from Xcode on the device (a TestFlight build never shows the rating window; an Xcode build shows it every time, so this checks the timing, not the 60-day limit). Save a video to Photos. About a second after "Saved to Photos", the rating window ("Enjoying yPrompt?") should appear. Tapping Not Now is enough; ratings from a development build aren't submitted.
- [ ] Save, then tap Share straight away: the rating window should not appear over the share sheet.

**4. Pro after a failed payment** *(Xcode StoreKit testing; optional)*
- [ ] Run from Xcode with the scheme's `OnlineStore.storekit`. With the .storekit file open, turn on Editor › Enable Billing Grace Period and Editor › Enable Billing Retry on Renewal, and set a fast renewal rate (Editor › Default Subscription Renewal Rate). Start the Annual trial in the app.
- [ ] When the renewal fails, the app stays Pro during the grace period (no mark on saved videos). Once the grace period is over and the subscription is only in billing retry, Pro ends: saved videos carry the "Made with yPrompt" mark again. In Debug › StoreKit › Manage Transactions you can resolve the billing issue; Pro should come back.
- [ ] A normal Pro subscription (or Lifetime) stays Pro after restarting the app.
- *Not verified here: the menu names above are from recent Xcode versions; if they differ, the same switches are in the .storekit file's editor.*

**Also check:** after updating from 1.0.6, no What's New sheet appears. On a device still on 1.0.5 (or a Mac on 1.0.4), updating straight to 1.0.7 shows the 1.0.6 sheet (Reading Fonts, More Languages, Better with VoiceOver) once.
